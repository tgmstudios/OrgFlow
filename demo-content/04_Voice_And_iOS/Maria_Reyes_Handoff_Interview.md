# Maria Reyes: fictional voice handoff interview

Participants: Maria Reyes and Jordan Lee
Date: October 29, 2026 (staged example)
Recording consent: script only, not a real recorded interview
State: Reviewed practice text, awaiting procedure publication

Jordan: Maria, you've coordinated volunteers here for six years. What's the one thing people always ask you?

Maria: Where should I go on my first day? New volunteers arrive at eight thirty. A shift lead meets them at the blue welcome table by the side entrance.

Jordan: What should happen before they touch anything?

Maria: They meet an experienced buddy, learn where to wash their hands, see the exits and first aid kit, and understand our privacy rules. Then we show them one station.

Jordan: How do you put together a month's schedule?

Maria: I collect availability by the fifteenth and finalize the schedule by the twenty second. Trained shift leads go in first. I never leave a first-time volunteer alone at a station. Reminders go two days before each shift.

Jordan: What if someone cancels the day before distribution?

Maria: Check the trained waitlist and tell Sam if you cannot safely cover a station. Don't quietly change the public hours or share someone's number without permission.

Jordan: Is that all written down somewhere?

Maria: Most of it is in our welcome guide and monthly scheduling notes. My biggest worry is the small questions that never make it into those documents.

Jordan: What's still missing?

Maria: Who approves station consolidation when Sam is unavailable? And can we make the reminders easier for volunteers who need simpler directions? I don't know the final answer yet.

Jordan: We'll leave those as open questions. I'll review the draft, link the sources, and publish only after Sam confirms.

Maria: Perfect. I can leave, and the next person doesn't have to start from nothing.
