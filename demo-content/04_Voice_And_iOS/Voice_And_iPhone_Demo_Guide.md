# Voice and iPhone demo guide

The latest app source includes web Chat and ElevenLabs Voice, consented transcript drafting, and iOS Ask, Capture, Handoff and Library. The newest iOS app has not been verified on a physical device.

## Web voice demonstration
Ask naturally: "What time does a new volunteer arrive for their first shift?"
Expected result based on the current Volunteer Welcome guide: 8:30 a.m. and the blue welcome table. If a real cited answer appears, open the source. If it fails, do not fabricate the interface outcome.

For the transfer interview, say: "Maya, what donor work might Priya miss after you leave?"
Continue with: "Who is responsible for a gift over one thousand dollars?" and "What questions are still unanswered?"
End the voice session, review the transcript, explicitly approve sending it to the drafting agent and show the saved draft. Publication remains a separate human action.

## iPhone Capture
Dictate: "New volunteers arrive at eight thirty. The shift lead meets them at the blue welcome table, introduces a buddy, and reviews safety and confidentiality."

## iPhone Handoff
Dictate: "Priya takes over donor acknowledgements after Maya leaves. The receipt goes out within twenty four hours. A personal thank-you is due within two working days. Gifts above one thousand dollars require a director call unless the contact preference says not to call."

## iPhone Library
Film the Knowledge, Files, Upcoming, and Checklists tabs if the app genuinely loads this data. Use a phone recording and hide all real notifications. Do not simulate an untested live account connection as real footage.

## Filming note
The app does not automatically publish drafted playbooks. Keep the review step visible. Do not display any real user's Gmail, Drive, staff data, authentication codes or API keys.
