# Harvest Dinner Fundraiser: Event Runbook
Prepared by: Maya Chen and Jordan Lee
Event date: October 24, 2026
Venue: Fictional pantry community hall, State College
Status: Current draft for review

## Purpose
The Harvest Dinner brings fictional supporters and volunteers together to raise awareness of the pantry's work. The event is a sample scenario intended for an OrgFlow product demonstration. No public invitation, ticket sale, event registration, or payment processing is live.

## Event facts for staff
Doors open at 5:30 p.m. The program begins at 6:15 p.m. The event wraps by 8:00 p.m. The fictional planning assumption is 80 guests, including six designated volunteers and four assigned staff members. Admission examples are $25 for an individual ticket and $45 for a pair. These amounts are samples, not actual sale offers.

## Before the event
October 12: finalize room layout and accessibility routes.
October 15: collect volunteer assignments and accessibility requirements.
October 19: confirm the guest count and dietary information with the catering lead.
October 21: review the program script and confirm the donation processing plan.
October 23: prepare name tags, wayfinding signs, sealed receipt envelopes, and the volunteer briefing.

## Assigned roles
Jordan Lee coordinates volunteer shifts and accessible check-in. Sam Rivera manages room safety, setup and the food service handoff. Maya Chen oversees the donor welcome and acknowledgement queue. Priya Patel assists at the fundraising desk and records follow-up actions without exposing donor information publicly.

## Event-day timeline
3:30 p.m. Staff walkthrough, first aid location, and access check.
4:00 p.m. Setup volunteers arrive and assemble signage.
5:00 p.m. Quick safety and privacy briefing for all helpers.
5:30 p.m. Doors open and guests check in.
6:15 p.m. The program begins with a short welcome.
6:40 p.m. A pantry staff member gives an approved overview.
7:10 p.m. Dinner and informal conversations.
7:45 p.m. Thank volunteers and share next steps.
8:00 p.m. Close, reconcile materials, and begin cleanup.

## Guest privacy and photographs
Do not collect unnecessary guest information on open clipboards. Ask for consent before photographs. Do not name pantry recipients in event remarks or imply that anyone featured is a client. Only the authorized Development lead may reconcile contributions.

## After the event
By October 26, send a personal thank-you to volunteers and sponsors. By October 27, reconcile all ticket examples and donations in the restricted register. By October 30, share an approved event recap without invented impact claims. Record what made check-in easy and what should change next year.

## The next person should know
The most fragile knowledge is the handoff between event volunteers and the donor acknowledgement queue. Jordan provides the final volunteer roster and Maya or Priya confirms donor contact preferences. Do not use the draft runbook as a claim that an actual event took place.
