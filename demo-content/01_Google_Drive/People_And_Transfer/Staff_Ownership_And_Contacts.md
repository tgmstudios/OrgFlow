# Staff Ownership and Role Coverage
Updated October 11, 2026
Status: Internal example. All names and roles are fictional.

Maria Reyes, outgoing Volunteer Coordinator, leaves October 31, 2026 after six years. Maria contributed the Volunteer Welcome and Monthly Scheduling guides.

Jordan Lee, incoming Volunteer Coordinator, takes the recurring schedule and first-shift onboarding. Jordan is also the current reviewer of those operational guides, and Sam Rivera provides backup coverage.

Sam Rivera, Operations Manager, owns Building Safety and Cold Chain, board meeting preparation, and escalations if staffing drops below the usual distribution coverage.

Maya Chen, Development Director, leaves November 15, 2026. Maya owns donor acknowledgements, quarterly grant reporting, and development follow-up.

Priya Patel, Development Associate and incoming acting development lead, will assume Maya's donor and grant responsibilities after human review of the handoff guide.

## Ownership risk
Two transitions overlap during a busy month. Maria's onboarding shortcuts should become an approved volunteer process before October 31. Maya's donor and grant work needs a named backup and a tested successor checklist before November 15. A documented draft is not the same as a reviewed and published procedure.

## Safe contact handling
The demonstration does not include personal phone numbers, employee identifiers, real email inboxes, or private addresses. If the live product requires contact details, create dedicated test identities and use example.org only for static mock data, not as an email delivery destination.
