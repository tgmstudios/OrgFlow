# Building Safety and Cold Chain
Owner: Sam Rivera, Operations Manager
Approved: September 28, 2026
Review due: December 28, 2026

## Why we maintain this procedure
A food pantry has to protect visitors, volunteers, and the integrity of distributed items. This fictional guide gives staff a clear escalation structure rather than expecting every volunteer to improvise. It is a demo document, not a substitute for local food safety regulations or professionally approved policies.

## Opening safety walk
Before volunteers arrive, Sam or an assigned lead checks that exits are clear, handwashing materials are stocked, spill supplies are accessible, and the first aid kit has not been moved. The team also checks that electrical cords do not cross walking paths and that the accessible entrance is unobstructed.

## Chilled item handling
Only designated staff record chilled storage readings. Store foods according to actual supplier guidance and the organization's approved food handling policy. If a reading, label, or storage history is unclear, hold the item and contact Sam. Do not rely on a guessed temperature threshold or improvisation from an AI answer.

## What a volunteer should do
1. Stop the task if an item looks unsafe, a person is hurt, or access is blocked.
2. Keep other volunteers from using the affected area without creating a new hazard.
3. Notify the shift lead and Sam Rivera.
4. Record the time, broad location, and what was observed without personal medical details.
5. Follow the trained staff member's instructions.
6. Resume the task only when authorized.

## Confidentiality
Incident notes do not belong on open volunteer schedules. For an injury or visitor concern, record only what trained staff require. Do not email identifiable information to a public list. The example incident log is entirely fictional.

## Examples
A box arrives with a broken seal. A volunteer sets it aside in the designated hold area and calls the shift lead. A storage label is missing. The volunteer does not distribute the item until authorized staff verify its history. A spill appears beside the entrance. The lead temporarily directs traffic around it and requests cleanup assistance.

## Changes and reporting
Sam reviews each incident for lessons and updates the procedure after any confirmed policy change. The current version lives in the approved knowledge base. Old printed copies should be removed when superseded.
