# Monthly Volunteer Scheduling
Document owner: Jordan Lee, Volunteer Coordinator
Approved: October 4, 2026
Next review: January 4, 2027

## The recurring rhythm
Volunteer availability is collected by the 15th of the month. The coordinator sends the same availability form to active volunteers, including Wednesday and Friday distribution, Tuesday packing, and special event options. Use internal volunteer IDs in shared spreadsheets when possible.

The schedule is finalized by the 22nd. Place trained shift leads first, then returning volunteers, and then newcomers with a buddy. Confirm the accessible entrance and any private accommodations individually. The schedule does not contain household names, confidential volunteer notes, or emergency contact details.

Send shift reminders two days before each shift. Each reminder states the date, arrival time, entrance, shift lead, and a simple way to reply. If someone cancels, contact the waitlist first. Ask Sam Rivera to revise station coverage before announcing changes.

## Staffing standards
Distribution runs Wednesday and Friday from 9:00 a.m. to noon. The shift lead arrives at 8:15 a.m., new volunteers at 8:30 a.m., and returning volunteers at 8:45 a.m.

A typical distribution plan includes one shift lead at check-in, two volunteers on produce, two on dry goods, one supervised helper at chilled storage, and one flexible carrying assistant. The shift lead confirms headcount before opening. With fewer than five confirmed volunteers, Sam decides whether to consolidate stations or find additional help.

## Monthly procedure
1. On the 12th, prepare the next month's availability request and check the active roster.
2. By the 15th, distribute the form and record responses.
3. On the 18th, follow up with shift leads and review missing coverage.
4. By the 22nd, publish the draft schedule for staff review.
5. Send the final confirmations with arrival times and parking details.
6. Send reminders two days before each assigned shift.
7. Track cancellations, arrivals, and improvement notes after each distribution.
8. At month's end, review scheduling gaps with Sam and update this guide if needed.

## Example of a change
A volunteer cancels on Monday for Wednesday distribution. Jordan checks the waitlist, invites one trained returning volunteer, and informs Sam if the gap remains. Do not independently move the opening time or disclose another volunteer's contact information.
