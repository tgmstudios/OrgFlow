# 90-second script and storyboard

Draft for a demo with connected content, generated playbooks, employee approval, source-grounded answers and role handoff. Verify these before narration lock. If a capability is missing, rewrite the corresponding line and use the working-flow fallback in `04-team-handoff.md`.

The table allocates exactly 90 seconds. Narration is intentionally sparse enough to allow reading and sound design. Record naturally, time each paragraph and edit the words if needed rather than speeding up the voice. Use the table's timings as an editorial plan, not a claim that the unrecorded narration already fits.

## On-screen text

Keep explanatory titles short; captions may reproduce the narration separately.

- Opening: “When people leave, what happens to what they know?”
- Reveal: “Org Flow”
- Capture: “Existing knowledge → usable playbooks”
- Review: “Reviewed by your team”
- Ask: “Ask. Find. Keep moving.”
- Transfer: “A handoff with context”
- End: “People move on. Your mission keeps moving.”

Do not include unverified customer counts, time-saving percentages, security certifications or claims of universal integration support. Target benefits can be framed as the product's purpose without inventing measured outcomes.

## Recording the voice

Read like you are showing a teammate something useful: warm, direct, confident. Pause after the opening question and before the brand name. Use a quiet room and the best microphone already available. Record two full takes plus sentence pickups. Select the stronger take before polishing the visuals.

Record paragraph by paragraph if timing is difficult. Leave a little silence between phrases; it makes editing easier. Preserve the original recording separately from the cleaned version.
