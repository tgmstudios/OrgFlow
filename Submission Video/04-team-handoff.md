## If some features are unfinished

- Integration unfinished: demonstrate the working upload/import path; say “Start with your team's documents.” Do not display unimplemented provider logos as connected.
- Approval unfinished: remove the approval narration and use that slot for a longer look at the actual playbook. Do not imitate an approval state.
- Citations unfinished: remove “with context they can check” and the source-opening shot. Verify what grounds the answer before keeping that claim.
- Handoff unfinished: show the existing playbook's value for the next coordinator; label a future handoff concept “Planned” if you include it at all.
- Only one feature works: give it the full demonstration block. Structure the film as problem → working input → working output → benefit → brief roadmap → close.

Lock factual claims before the final voice take. Shorten the pitch to match the functioning product when needed.
